# Introduction film credits

CosmicPulse of public-domain NASA footage from the NASA Image and Video Library
(https://images.nasa.gov). NASA material is generally not subject to U.S. copyright. **NASA does not
endorse CosmicPulse.** Astronauts shown are real NASA/partner crew in official footage, not the
fictional CosmicPulse demo crew.
